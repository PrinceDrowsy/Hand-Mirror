# Hand Mirror - Character Creation Expansion

An expanded character creator for Echoes of Aincrad, in the spirit of RaceMenu, built so other mods can plug into it.

Work in progress: nothing to install yet.

## Plans

- **More slots:** new eyes, hair and other parts are added as new options instead of replacing the game's own, so part mods stop overwriting each other.
- **Extras:** ears, horns and tails as their own category, with ear sliders built on the ear bones every head already has.
- **A free color wheel** for every color, skin included, with one list of recent colors.
- **Body:** wider slider ranges, height, real body weight, a shared standard for body replacers, and heels that keep feet on the ground.
- **Any outfit on any body**, refitted to your shape.
- **Overlays:** freckles, tattoos, war paint and scars, placed anywhere.
- **Free-placed accessories** and **physics sliders** for hair, cloth and body.
- **Change your look any time** with the hand mirror item, and **Two Faces**: a beta avatar first, then your real self at the hand mirror scene.
- **Presets and share codes.**

## Adding parts today

Until Hand Mirror adds parts at runtime, new pupils, eyebrows, eyelines, noses and jaws can already be added as new creator options with [Cardinal](https://github.com/PrinceDrowsy/Echoes-of-Aincrad-Mods/tree/main/cardinal)'s `new part` template.
